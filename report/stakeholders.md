# Actor goals

| actor | goal |
| --- | --- |
| Member | To book a desk or room, change their times, and know for sure if their spot is actually locked in |
| Visitor / Guest | To get short-term access to the space without seeing any private info about the members |
| Reception staff | To check people in, fix the system if someone grabs a different desk, and stop double-bookings |
| Workspace manager | To see how busy the place is, track broken gear or no-shows, and make sure rooms have the right equipment when booked |
| Organisation administrator | To manage regular group bookings and update dates for things like holidays |
| Temporary cover staff | To help people arriving at the front desk without seeing private business details |
| Facilities staff | To check on faults and know if a room can still be booked even if something inside it is broken |
| Access system | To let members check into the building |
| Accounting system | To handle the final bills and keep track of extra charges |
