# Actor goals

| actor | goal |
| --- | --- |
| Member | To book a desk or room, change their times, and know for sure if their spot is actually locked in |
| Visitor / Guest | To get short-term access to the space without seeing any private info about the members |
| Reception staff | To check people in, fix the system if someone grabs a different desk, and stop double-bookings |
| Workspace manager | To see how busy the place is, track broken gear or no-shows, and make sure rooms have the right equipment when booked |
| Organisation administrator | To manage regular group bookings and update dates for things like holidays |
| Temporary cover staff | To help people arriving at the front desk without seeing private business details |
| Facilities staff | To check on faults and know if a room can still be booked even if something inside it is broken |
| Access system | Provides member check-in information to the system |
| Accounting system | Receives usage information from the system and produces the final bills |

## Stakeholder analysis

| Stakeholder | Goal | Concern | Influence | Interaction |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Workspace manager | Run the workspace efficiently and keep members satisfied | "A free room is not useful if the equipment the member requested is unavailable." | **High**: agrees plan exceptions and decides how the workspace runs | Views occupancy, conflicts, provisional bookings, no-shows and faults |
| Members | Have the space and resources their plan gives them when they need them | "I need to know whether a booking is confirmed or only being held temporarily." | **Medium**: their satisfaction affects renewals, but they don't set policy | Makes and changes bookings, views their own bookings and public resource information |
| Reception staff | Keep arrivals and daily running smooth | "People move to another desk and tell us later, so occupancy is not always accurate." | **Medium**: daily user who keeps the records up to date | Checks people in, handles unexpected arrivals and updates records |
| Facilities staff | Keep the building and equipment usable | "A fault may limit a room without making the whole room unusable." | **Medium**: knows the real state of resources and what can still be used | Logs faults and maintenance and records which resources are limited or removed |
| Organisation administrators | Keep their organisation's regular bookings working | "Our recurring booking changes during holidays, but old dates remain in the calendar." | **Medium**: represents a whole organisation's bookings | Manages recurring bookings and updates dates |
| Temporary cover staff | Cover reception and handle arrivals | "I need to handle arrivals without seeing every member's commercial information." | **Low**: short-term users with limited access | Handles arrivals using the views their role allows |
| Visitors | Get short-term access to the workspace | Assumed: being refused or delayed because it is unclear who can approve them, or having member details exposed | **Low**: occasional users with no say in how the system works | Takes part in guest visits and short-term access, and may book selected resources |
| Accounting personnel | Bill members correctly | Assumed: usage information arriving late, incomplete or wrong, which leads to billing errors | **Medium**: depends on the usage data that the system produces | None directly |
| Organisations using workspaces | Have a reliable place for their staff to work | Assumed: staff losing access or bookings, or other organisations seeing their activity | **Medium**: Their opinion matters commercially | None directly |
