# Scenario interpretation

## Organisation
Tralee Enterprise Workspace provides office space to customers that can be booked by either individuals or multiple people. Different customers get different levels of access but frequent visitors can book specific resources.

## Problems
The business currently manages bookings, access, and equipment issues through spreadsheets, calendars, and emails. 
Due to this, conflicting reservations occur and information becomes unclear about customer access, capacity, and availability of services.

## Members
**Members may be:**
- Individuals
- Linked to an organisation

**Members can have plans/memberships which affect:**
- Desk hours
- Meeting room allowance
- Access times
- Guest privileges
- Special equipment

Exceptions can apply but are not documented consistently.

**Newly registered members are given:**
- Introduction/walkaround tour
- Arranged initial access

**Membership status types:**
- Active
- Suspended
- Awaiting renewal
- Ending

Regardless of membership status, the booking calendar may still contain future reservations by the user.

### Visitors and guests
In some cases, customers need temporary access as they aren't intended to be members. They should never come in contact with member details.

*Problem:* 
Staff are unsure whether the member, receptionist, or manager should approve different types of guest visits.

## Resources and bookings

**A booking covers:**
- Desk
- OR room
- OR equipment
- OR a combination

**Rooms have:**
- Capacity
- Layout
- Accessibility 
- Equipment information

**Equipment:**
- Can be reserved
- May need repairs from time to time

A room can be available while the required equipment is reserved somewhere else or away on repair.

**Bookings may be:**
- Single
- Recurring
- Provisional

*Problem:* 
Sometimes bookings are made provisionally, and staff disagree on how long provisional bookings should block others from booking the room.

## Daily operations and service issues

**Members check in through an access system or reception.**

*Occasionally against procedure:*
- Customers arrive without a visible booking
- Bring an unexpected guest
- Use a different free desk

In this case, staff sometimes allow this and update the record later.

**Faults that can occur:**
- Unavailable internet
- Damaged furniture
- Missing cables
- Inaccessible rooms

These faults can affect one resource, several bookings, or the whole site.
*Problem:* Staff need a reliable way to know if resources can still be used with limitations.

**Bookings may be:**
- Relocated
- Shortened
- Cancelled
(Due to maintenance or community events closing part of the workspace).

*Problem:*
Messages about closures currently differ between emails, signs, and direct calls.

## Charges, usage and information

**Charges may occur from:**
- Excess usage past plan limits
- Visitor access 
- Use of specialist equipment

*Problem:*
Staff need reliable usage information to pass on for charging.

**Information views needed:**
- The workspace manager needs visibility of occupancy, recurring conflicts, provisional bookings, no-shows, faults, and resources removed from service.
- Members need to see their own bookings and public resource info (but not other organisations' activity).
- Different staff (reception, facilities, temp cover) require different access views. Temp staff must handle arrivals without seeing commercial info.

# Uncertainty and rules register

**Clarification Questions for Stakeholders:**
- Which plan rules may be overridden and who has the authority to approve exceptions?
- What is the maximum time a provisional or recurring booking can reserve resources before being confirmed or dropped?
- How should future bookings in the calendar be treated when a membership is suspended or ends?
- Who exactly (member, receptionist, or manager) is allowed to approve guests and temporary access?
- How do no-shows and unrecorded resource changes officially affect a member's usage limits?
- What are the exact rules for when a partially usable resource may continue to be booked?
- Which specific booking, membership, and charge information is visible to each individual staff role?
