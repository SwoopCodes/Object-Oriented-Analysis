# Scenario interpretation

## Organisation
Tralee Enterprise Workspace provides office space to customers that can be booked by either individuals or multiple people. Different customers get different levels of access but occasional visitors can book specific resources.

The business currently manages bookings, access, and equipment issues through spreadsheets, calendars, and emails. 
Conflicting reservations occur and information becomes unclear about customer access, capacity, and availability of services.

## Members
**Members may be:**
- Individuals
- Linked to an organisation

**Members can have plans/memberships which affect:**
- Desk hours
- Meeting room allowance
- Access times
- Guest privileges
- Special equipment

Exceptions can apply but are not documented consistently.

**Newly registered members are given:**
- Induction
- Arranged initial access

**Membership status types:**
- Active
- Suspended
- Awaiting renewal
- Ending

Regardless of membership status, the booking calendar may still contain future reservations by the user.

### Visitors and guests
In some cases, customers need temporary access as they aren't intended to be members. They should never come in contact with member details.

*Problem:* 
Staff are unsure whether the member, receptionist, or manager should approve different types of guest visits.

## Resources and bookings

**A booking covers:**
- Desk
- OR room
- OR equipment
- OR a combination

**Rooms have:**
- Capacity
- Layout
- Accessibility 
- Equipment information

**Equipment:**
- Can be reserved
- May need repairs from time to time

A room can be available while the required equipment is reserved somewhere else or away on repair.

**Bookings may be:**
- Single
- Recurring
- Provisional



## Daily operations and service issues

**Members check in through an access system or reception.**

*Occasionally:*
- Customers arrive without a visible booking
- Bring an unexpected guest
- Use a different free desk

In this case, staff sometimes allow this and update the record later.

**Faults that can occur:**
- Unavailable internet
- Damaged furniture
- Missing cables
- Inaccessible rooms

These faults can affect one resource, several bookings, or the whole site.

**Bookings may be:**
- Relocated
- Shortened
- Cancelled
(Due to maintenance or community events closing part of the workspace).


## Charges, usage and information

**Charges may occur from:**
- Excess usage past plan limits
- Visitor access 
- Use of specialist equipment

**Information views needed:**
- The workspace manager needs visibility of occupancy, recurring conflicts, provisional bookings, no-shows, faults, and resources removed from service.
- Members need to see their own bookings and public resource info (but not other organisations' activity).
- Different staff (reception, facilities, temp cover) require different access views. Temp staff must handle arrivals without seeing commercial info.

## Problems and consequences

- **P01** Conflicting reservations: members may arrive to find their resource already taken, and staff have to sort it out on the spot.

- **P02** Information about access, capacity and availability is kept in several places: staff may give different answers to the same question and spend time checking.

- **P03** Plan exceptions are agreed but not documented consistently: similar members may be treated differently, and staff may not know what was agreed.

- **P04** Staff are unsure who approves guest visits: guests may be delayed, or let in without the right approval.

- **P05** Staff disagree on how long provisional bookings should block a room: rooms may be held longer than needed, and members can't tell where they stand. The member said, "I need to know whether a booking is confirmed or only being held temporarily."

- **P06** A room can be free while its equipment is reserved elsewhere or under repair: a booking may look fine but not be usable. The manager said, "A free room is not useful if the equipment the member requested is unavailable."

- **P07** Faults can limit a resource without closing it: staff may block something that could still be used, or allow a booking that can't really go ahead. The facilities staff member said, "A fault may limit a room without making the whole room unusable."

- **P08** People move desks or arrive without a booking and the record is updated later: occupancy is likely to be out of date. The receptionist said, "People move to another desk and tell us later, so occupancy is not always accurate."

- **P09** Recurring bookings change around holidays but the old dates stay in the calendar: resources may look booked when they are not. The organisation administrator said, "Our recurring booking changes during holidays, but old dates remain in the calendar."

- **P10** Closure messages differ between emails, signs and calls: members may get conflicting information about moved, shortened or cancelled bookings.

- **P11** Staff need reliable usage information for charging: charges may be missed or queried if usage is not recorded accurately.

Overall, staff spend more time checking and correcting records, and members are less sure about what they can use.

## Desired Outcomes
The scenario states that *"Staff want to reduce conflicting reservations and provide clearer information about access, capacity and service availability."* The outcomes below describe what stakeholders want to be true after the change.

- Members and staff experience far fewer conflicting reservations.
- Members can tell whether their booking is confirmed or only being held.
- Staff can trust that occupancy and availability reflect what is actually happening in the workspace.
- Members who need a room together with equipment can find out whether both are available.
- Staff share a consistent understanding of what each member's plan allows, including any agreed exceptions.
- Staff know whether a faulty resource can still be used and under what limitations.
- Members receive the same, clear message when bookings are relocated, shortened or cancelled.
- The manager has dependable information about how the workspace is being used, and the accounting side receives reliable usage information for charging.
- Visitors can gain temporary access without exposure to member information, and staff only see the information their role needs.
