# CA2 design - Workspace Management System

## Product Goal

Give Tralee Enterprise Workspace and its members one reliable, shared record of memberships, bookings and resource availability, so that conflicting reservations stop, everyone knows what space and equipment they can actually use, and usage is recorded accurately enough to charge for fairly.
