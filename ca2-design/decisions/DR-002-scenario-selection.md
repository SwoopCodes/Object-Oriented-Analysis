## Context

Each member wrote a CA1 scenario. The group needs one scenario for CA2. We need to record which one we use and why.

## Decision

Decided: 04/11/2026 (lecturer approval). Written: 26/11/2026.

We use Scenario 13 for CA2. The lecturer approved the group and scenario on 04/11/2026 (see DR-001).

## Rationale

- The CA1 files for Scenario 13 are in the repo. UC01 and UC05 build on them.
- The scenario has booking rules and conflicts we can test. Five Gherkin scenarios (SC01 to SC05) cover them.
