# Scenario interpretation

## Organisation
Tralee enterprise is a company which provides office space to customers that can be booked by either individuals or multiple people. Different customers get different levels of access but frequent visitors can book specific resources

## problems
The business currently manages:

- bookings
- access
- equipment issues

through spreadsheets, calanders and emails

Due to this, conflicting reservations occur and information becomes unclear about customer access, capacity and availability of services

---

# Members

**Members may be:**
- Individuals
- linked to an organisation

**Members can have plans/memberships which affect:**
- desk hours
- meeting room allowance
- access times
- guest privilleges
- special equipment

Exceptions can apply and be **enforced by the workspace manager** but not document consistently

**newly registered members are given:**
- introduction/walkaround tour
- arranged initial access

**membership status types:**
- Active
- Suspended
- awaiting renewal
- ending

regardless of membership status **booking calander may still contain future reservations** by the user

### **visitors and guests**

In some cases, customers need temporary access as they aren't intended to be members
They should **never** come in contact with member details

*uncertainty*
What are the different types of guest visits

*problem*
Staff are unsure wether the member, receptionist or manager should approve the guest visitors

*assumption*
The member should have an option to assign a guest/visitor themselves which would become visible to the receptionist who would approve it

# resources and bookings

**booking covers:**
- Desk
- OR room
- OR equipment
- OR all of the above

**rooms have**
- capacity
- layout
- accessability 
- equipment information

**equipment**
- not fixed to any room/location
- can be reserved
- may need repairs from time to time

A room *can be available* while the rooms required equipment can be reserved somewhere else or away on repair

**bookings may be:**
- Singular
- recurring

*problem* 
sometimes bookings are made *provisionally* which sometimes staff disagree with as customers book random rooms to do it

*solution*
>answer required before submission

# Daily operations and service issues

**members check in through access system or reception**

*ocasionally against procedure*
- customers arrive without prior booking 
- bring an unexpected guest
- use different desk

In this case, the receptionist would update the record

**things that should be checked when this happens**
> member appears without booking:
- capacity of free desks, room etc should be verified
- member should be booked in and noted

> bringing an unexpected guest
- guest must be booked in by receptionist
- membership plan and visitor limits should be checked to see if member is permitted to bring guest

> use different desk
- update desk number for booking
